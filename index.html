Reading · Alternative practice

Friendship grows through everyday choices.

Read the passage about trust, respect, and making room for differences. Then try both activities.

A little about friendshipReading passage · about 140 words

Friendship is more than having fun together. It grows through small, everyday actions. Good friends listen when someone needs to talk, keep their promises, and make room for each other’s ideas. They do not have to enjoy all the same things. One friend might love team sports while another would rather draw. Respecting these differences helps both people feel comfortable being themselves.

Friends sometimes disagree or make mistakes. A strong friendship does not mean never arguing; it means speaking honestly, trying to understand, and saying sorry when needed. Trust takes time to build, so friends protect it by keeping private things private and being reliable. They also need space for family, school, and other interests. Giving each other that space can make their time together even better. Friendship works best when both people share care, respect, and support.

✿A thought to keep

Good friends don’t have to be alike. They listen, respect differences, and show up for each other.
